# Credits for the demo's photos

The fifty street photos that `deploy/compose/demo.sh` downloads, and that appear in the
screenshots of the README, come from the validation set of
[Open Images V5](https://storage.googleapis.com/openimages/web/index.html). Each is listed
there under [CC BY 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/); the list below
names each photo's title, its page and its author, as that licence asks. The photos are
shown as they are, with boxes drawn over them by the dashboard. The boxes are Open Images'
own annotations, by Google LLC under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/);
the demo moves and renames a few of them on purpose in its first release, to have a
review to show in its second.

The two clips of the file dataset (`video/trolley-crossing.mp4`, `video/bus-stop.mp4`) are
a slow pan across `frames/frame-000.jpg` and `frames/frame-037.jpg`, made by the demo.

The photos are not part of this repository: the demo fetches them from Open Images'
bucket and checks each against the hash in `street-scenes.tsv`. Open Images gives no
warranty that a photo's licence is as listed; if one of these is yours and listed wrongly,
say so and it goes.
